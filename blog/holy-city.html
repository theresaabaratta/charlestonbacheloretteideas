Charleston Bachelorette Ideas

Downtown Charleston packs rooftops, carriage-lined streets, harbor cruises and some of the best food in the South into a few walkable blocks. Here is the short version of where to take the bride.

1Nightlife Downtown Starts on the Rooftops

Kick off with sunset drinks at The Vendue, whose rooftop bar looks over the harbor and the French Quarter. From there the night usually drifts to King Street. Prohibition brings Roaring Twenties cocktails and live music on weekends, while Uptown Social has a big rooftop deck, games and a crowd that stays late. If your group wants a mapped-out route, the King Street bar crawl guide on Party Charleston lays out stops block by block so nobody gets lost between bars.

2Eats Worth Planning the Weekend Around

Charleston's food scene is half the reason groups pick it. For a sit-down dinner with a southern edge, book Husk or Poogan's Porch, a Queen Street house known for fried chicken and biscuits. Steak lovers can go big at Halls Chophouse, and 167 Raw is the move for oysters and lobster rolls if you can handle the line. For a casual morning-after, Sweet Palm Coffee & Food serves coffee, breakfast plates and wraps that fix a long night.

3Boat Charters on Charleston Harbor

Getting the group out on the water is a highlight for almost every downtown bachelorette. Schooner Pride runs sunset sails on a tall ship with the bar open on board, and Charleston Harbor Tours offers harbor cruises past Fort Sumter and the Battery. For a private boat with a captain, sandbar stops and your own playlist, this private bachelorette charter picks up at Seabreeze Marina downtown.

4Candle Making for a Keepsake Afternoon

A candle class gives everyone something to take home besides a hangover. Candlefish on King Street runs pour-your-own workshops in a shop lined with hundreds of scents. Edmund's Oast Brewing Co. hosts themed candle nights where the beer is part of the deal, and Classpop's Charleston candle classes can set up a private session for just your group.

5Kayaking Through the Lowcountry Marsh

A morning paddle is the reset button between big nights. Coastal Expeditions leads guided kayak tours through tidal creeks where dolphins regularly show up, and beginners are welcome. Book an early slot in summer to beat the heat and the afternoon storms.

6Picnics With Full Setups

Styled picnic companies bring the rugs, low tables, pillows, flowers and charcuterie to a spot you pick, whether that is White Point Garden, Hampton Park or the backyard of your rental. Two good options to browse are this boho luxury picnic and a full setup with a charcuterie spread. Order bride-to-be signage ahead and you will get the best photos of the trip.

7Yoga to Balance the Weekend

Plan one slow morning. Holy Cow Yoga has heated and gentle classes and can work with groups, and Satsang Yoga Charleston is a calm, well-reviewed studio downtown. Ask either about a private class so the whole crew can laugh through it together.

The Short Guide to Charleston

Friday: Check in, rooftop drinks at The Vendue, then dinner on Queen Street and a slow walk down King.

Saturday: Kayak or yoga in the morning, picnic or harbor sail in the afternoon, then Michael at the rental before the bars.

Sunday: Brunch and coffee, a candle class if flights allow, and one last group photo on Rainbow Row.
