Isle of Palms Bachelorette Ideas

Isle of Palms is the polished beach island twenty minutes from downtown, with oceanfront bars, a busy marina and wide quiet beaches. It suits groups that want resort vibes without losing the party.

1Nightlife Right on the Ocean

The Windjammer is the island's legendary beach bar, with live bands and a deck that opens onto the sand. Next door, Coconut Joe's has a rooftop for sunset drinks, and Islander 71 at the marina pours cocktails over the Intracoastal. For a downtown night, Party Charleston's bachelorette section has the plan.

2Eats From the Marina to Breach Inlet

The Boathouse at Breach Inlet serves seafood with sunset views over the inlet, and Morgan Creek Grill is a marina favorite for a long lunch on the water. Acme Lowcountry Kitchen handles big shrimp-and-grits brunches, and Long Island Cafe is the cozy local pick for dinner.

3Boat Charters From the IOP Marina

The Isle of Palms Marina is the jumping-off point for boat days to nearby sandbars and Capers Island. This private captained charter picks up right at the marina and anchors at a sandbar so the group can swim and float.

4Candle Making Between Beach Days

Candle classes are a quick drive away in Mount Pleasant and downtown. Check Classpop's candle making classes for dates or set up a private session for your crew.

5Kayaking the Back Creeks

Barrier Island Eco Tours runs kayak and boat tours out of the IOP marina into the creeks behind the island, and Coastal Expeditions offers guided paddles nearby. Dolphins are common, so keep the phones handy.

6Picnics on the Beach

Book a styled picnic with low tables, pillows and florals at a public beach access, or have it set up at your rental. This luxury picnic setup comes with a charcuterie spread and delivers to you.

7Yoga With an Ocean Breeze

Island Yoga is the local studio, and many IOP rentals are big enough for a private class on the deck. Book a sunrise flow on the beach to start the big day calmly.

The Short Guide to Isle of Palms

Friday: Arrive, sunset drinks at Coconut Joe's, then live music at The Windjammer.

Saturday: Beach yoga, a sandbar boat charter from the marina, then Michael at the beach house.

Sunday: Brunch at Acme Lowcountry Kitchen and a long beach walk before checkout.
