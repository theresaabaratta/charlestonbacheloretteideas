Folly Beach Bachelorette Ideas

Folly is the barefoot, surf-town side of Charleston. Rooftop bars, tacos, sandbars and sunset pier walks make it perfect for a bride who would rather wear flip-flops than heels.

Entertainment by Michael

Folly Beach is part of Michael's regular stomping ground, and beach houses on East and West Ashley Avenue are a perfect setting for the show. Michael comes to your rental, performs for up to 90 minutes and charges $200, paid on arrival with cash, Venmo or Cash App. Brunch or late night both work, and last-minute weekends are no problem. Book with Michael At Your Place or call (843) 360-9438.

1Nightlife Clusters on Center Street

Almost everything happens within a few blocks of Center Street. Rita's Seaside Grille has a big rooftop and frozen drinks steps from the sand, and Lowlife Bar has a second-floor deck with ocean views. Chico Feo is the island's open-air hangout with live music and a backyard full of locals. When the group wants the full Charleston scene, Party Charleston's party scene guide covers downtown.

2Eats With Sand Still on Your Feet

Lost Dog Cafe is the island's breakfast institution, with dog photos on the walls and huge plates. Taco Boy handles lunch and margaritas, and Bert's Market is open around the clock for snacks, drinks and beach supplies. For a seated seafood dinner, Rita's and Lowlife both double as good dining stops.

3Boat Charters to the Sandbars

Folly's tidal river and nearby sandbars are perfect for a boat day. Tideline Tours runs three-hour private BYOB charters with a lily pad float, and it is woman-owned with mostly female captains. Pack a cooler, sunscreen and a waterproof speaker and spend the afternoon floating.

4Candle Making Near the Island

Folly itself is all about the beach, but candle classes are a short drive away on James Island and Johns Island, often hosted at breweries. Browse Charleston candle making classes for dates or book a private pour for the group.

5Kayaking and Paddleboarding the Folly River

Charleston Outdoor Adventures runs guided kayak tours on the Folly River with regular dolphin sightings. For a twist, Charleston SUP Safaris offers paddleboard tours and even SUP yoga on the water.

6Beach Picnics Done Right

A styled beach picnic is the Folly photo op. Reserve chairs and umbrellas through Folly Beach Chair Company, or book a full setup like this boho luxury picnic. Check the town's beach rules first, since glass and alcohol are not allowed on the sand.

7Yoga on the Sand

Serenity Tree Yoga holds donation-based classes right on the beach, plus a monthly Glow Yoga session with glow sticks around the new moon. Bring towels instead of mats and finish with a swim.

The Short Guide to Folly Beach

Friday: Tacos at Taco Boy, sunset on the pier and drinks on a Center Street rooftop.

Saturday: Beach yoga, an afternoon sandbar charter, then Michael at the beach house.

Sunday: Breakfast at Lost Dog Cafe and a final paddle on the Folly River.
